ctbench pipeline

Signal only

The score counts only posts that are on-topic, firsthand and not shill or bot. Crypto Twitter is mostly hype, so the signal is a minority of the volume. Small n means a noisier number.

Which token

Tickers collide. Posts are pulled and judged against the token's contract address, so $AI or $PUMP do not drag in every other project with the same symbol.

How it is read

Each post is read once by a Claude Code subagent following a written rulebook, with no keyword matching. Off-schema labels are normalized. Capped at 500 posts over seven days per token.

Counts toward the score

A holder, trader or builder saying what they think from real exposure - price, narrative, momentum, tech, tokenomics, community or safety.

Shown but not scored

Paid shills and airdrop or points farming - giveaway raids - templated copy-paste - reactions with no stated position.

Left out entirely

Posts about another project that shares the ticker - pure price screenshots with no take - reposted calls and hype with no firsthand use.
